Outstanding questions:

	To B2C_ or not to B2C_?
	
		The document from Keith refers to the need to use B2C_ variants "for a B2C customer".
		Somehow, over the years, I seem to have lost clarity on exactly what determines my need to call the B2C_ variants. How do we define "a B2C customer"? By CUSREF?

		Keith Willis, 19/07/2019

			"For us a B2C Customer is someone who doesn’t have Trade terms with DG, and therefore won’t have their own record on SLCUST.
			Orders for this type of customer get created against a cash account, these have references like C0996 and C0999.
			SALES_ORDER_ALLOC and B2C_SALES_ORDER_ALLOC point to the same process at the back end, PG.SALES_ORDER_ALLOC.WI.
			B2C_SALES_ORDER_ALLOC passes a parameter to the paragraph that SALES_ORDER_ALLOC does not pass.  
			In the paragraph this parameter being set means that it uses a different method to determine if there is free stock when attempting to auto allocate stock to a line, 
			this is because B2C lines can allocate stock from special B2C locations where stock transfers are involved.
 
			Initially B2C functionality also meant the ability to set prices and values on the order line.
			This actually trips if you pass in the value; if you don’t pass it in then Mertex calculates it, regardless of whether you called SALES_ORDER_ALLOC or B2C_SALES_ORDER_ALLOC.
 
			Additionally B2C functionality also meant the ability to pass in an order number, carriage and haulier values when creating an order.
			However this functionality actually trips when you pass in an order number of 7 or 9 characters beginning with a 9.
			In the past this is only something you’d do with B2C orders anyway.
