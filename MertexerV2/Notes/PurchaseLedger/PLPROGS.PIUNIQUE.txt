006     * REASON  : HANDLE EXISTING DOCUMENT NUMBERS BETTER -

008     *           JUST ALLOCATED.

010     *

021       IF RECORD<34> NE "" THEN GOTO 30

039     * V1.2-

041 20 *                                                     
042       IF GUI THEN

044       END ELSE
